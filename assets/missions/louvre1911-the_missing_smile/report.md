# The Missing Smile

*Steal back the smile that blinded you.*

Inspired by the 1911 theft of the Mona Lisa from the Louvre. Every name, place and object is invented.

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_Lisa

## Checks

- Level validity, story beats and real-name check: passed on attempt 1.
- Balance (300 seeded runs each, guard schedule tuned by code: 3 turns at his post, walking forward through his whole patrol): careful player wins 100%; rushing player wins cleanly 0%.
