# The Hollow Frames

*Take the canvas, find the truth, choose who pays*

Inspired by the 1990 theft from the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum in Boston. Every name, place and object is invented.

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabella_Stewart_Gardner_Museum_theft
