# Ten Layers Down

*ten locks, one tape, one old betrayal*

Inspired by the 2003 Antwerp Diamond Centre heist. Every name, place and object is invented.

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antwerp_diamond_heist

## Checks
- Built by code from the model's spec; door walls and the guard's route computed, not written by the model.
- Level validity, story beats and real-name check: passed on attempt 1.
- Balance (300 seeded runs each, guard schedule tuned by code: 3 turns at his post, walking forward through his whole patrol): careful player wins 100%; rushing player wins cleanly 0%.
