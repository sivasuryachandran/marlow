    The job

    You are Owl, once the best fixer in the city. Three years ago, on the night of the Fenwick job, the lights went out and you lost your sight. All you remember from that night is a sound: a heavy step, a drag, and keys.

    Marlow is your eyes: young, brave, the only one who still calls you “boss”. Your old partner Ines sends you both into the Hale Gallery for one ledger. “Don’t read it. It’s only numbers.”

    How to play

    1. Wear headphones. The whole picture is 3D sound: you can hear which direction the guard is in.
    2. Speak an order (hold Space, or just talk if the mic is hands-free), type it, or press a key in the playbook.
    3. Marlow tells you what she sees. You only know what she tells you and what you hear.
    4. Get the ledger from the office and exit through the lobby. Doors are noisy; the guard hears them. If he spots her he runs: you get exactly one turn to hide or leave the room.
    5. If you win, Marlow plays you the tape she found. Say “burn it” or “send it”.

    Things to say

    listen · what do you see · go to the hall · peek the left door quietly · hide · take the ledger · go back · exit
    who is Ines · where can I hide · what’s in the ledger

    Questions to Marlow are free: no turn passes. The engine, not the AI, decides what happens; the AI only understands what you said.
